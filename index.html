THE CAMBODIAN CRAFT JOURNEY

Born in Phnom Penh, Rooted in Passion

In 2009, a group of young Cambodian university graduates and architects gathered with one simple, audacious dream: to create a genuine specialty coffee culture in Cambodia that rivals the best in the world.

Back then, Phnom Penh drank dark-roasted robusta with sweet condensed milk on street corners. We believed Cambodian palates deserved to experience the nuanced floral, fruity, and chocolate notes of ethically sourced Arabica beans—served in architecturally striking spaces that inspire creativity, connection, and pride.

"We didn't just want to serve coffee; we wanted to craft a communal living room for Cambodia's dreamers, thinkers, and creators."
2009
First Store Opened

Our very first boutique opened on Street 214 in Phnom Penh with just 8 baristas.

2014
Mondulkiri Direct Trade

Partnered with indigenous Bunong coffee growers to plant Arabica at 850m elevation.

2018
BROWN Barista Academy

Established our training academy to certify SCA-standard Cambodian baristas and roasters.

2026
25+ Boutiques & Beyond

Serving over 15,000 cups daily across Phnom Penh and Siem Reap with 1,000+ local team members.
